Unlocking Organic Growth Opportunities at Regork¶

Introduction¶

Regork has identified organic products as a high-value growth opportunity within its product portfolio. Organic items not only generate strong margins for the company, but also align with increasing consumer interest in healthier and more sustainable food choices. As demand for wellness-oriented products continues to rise, understanding how to expand organic purchasing behavior becomes a strategic priority.

The central business question guiding this analysis is: How can Regork increase customer purchases of organic products? To answer this, our team examined three key dimensions of organic purchasing behavior: who to target, when to target them, and which promotional strategies are most effective.

Using transaction, demographic, and promotion data, we analyzed customer segments, seasonal patterns, co-purchase behaviors, and the effectiveness of different types of discounts. By identifying the right customer segments, optimal timing, and most impactful promotional tactics, this report aims to provide data-driven recommendations that will increase organic sales while strengthening long-term customer loyalty.

Ultimately, our goal is to help Regork strategically position organic products in a way that drives sustainable revenue growth while encouraging healthier purchasing habits among its customers.

def categorize_household(row):
    if row['is_promoted'] and row['subsequent_purchase_count'] > 0:
        return 'Promoted initial, repeated'
    elif row['is_promoted'] and row['subsequent_purchase_count'] == 0:
        return 'Promoted initial, no repeat'
    elif not row['is_promoted'] and row['subsequent_purchase_count'] > 0:
        return 'Non-promoted initial, repeated'
    else:
        return 'Non-promoted initial, no repeat'

Summary¶

Observations

  • Middle income class are the majority of our customers.
  • The peak of sales is during July.
  • The top co-purchased item with another organic products are bananas.
  • More than half people that buy promoted organic promotions at first, keep buying the same product later.

Recommendations

  • Target middle- income level huseholds
  • Time of the year: June to August to use
  • Create promotions about co purchases of organic products with bananas
  • Acquire first-time organic buyers
