🛡️ Asset Protection

Spendthrift provisions mean beneficiaries hold only a right to receive distributions:

  • Shielded from beneficiary creditors and lawsuits
  • Divorcing spouses cannot claim trust assets
  • Bankruptcy-proof for any individual beneficiary
  • No estate tax at each generational transfer

💰 Tax Efficiency

  • GST exemption planning bypasses estate tax
  • Probate bypass — faster, private transfer
  • Trust-level tax planning by institutional trustee
  • Single-pool structure avoids fractured ownership

📊 Phase 1 Investment Thesis

  • 70% growth equities during 20-yr accumulation
  • 30% alternatives: private credit, EAFE, REIT, timber
  • J.P. Morgan: EAFE +1.4%/yr over US over 10–15 yr horizon
  • No withdrawals maximize compounding in early decades

🏦 Governance

  • Institutional trustee manages per investment policy statement
  • Board sign-off required for large alternative allocations
  • Unitrust prevents pot-draining by any single beneficiary
  • Not divided as it passes through generations
