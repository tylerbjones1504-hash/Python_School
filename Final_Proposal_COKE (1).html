2. Project Description & Data Variables

For this project, I will investigate the operational efficiency and revenue drivers of the largest independent Coca-Cola bottler in the United States. In an inflationary environment, it is critical to distinguish between growth driven by price increases versus growth driven by actual consumer demand (volume).

Key Research Question:

How do changes in "Price/Mix" compare to "Physical Case Volume" in driving Net Sales growth over the last three fiscal years (2023–2025)?

Data Variables to be Analyzed:

  • Net Sales: Total annual revenue.
  • Physical Case Volume: The actual number of beverage cases sold (found in the Results of Operations section).
  • Cost of Sales (COGS): Used to calculate and visualize gross margin trends.
  • Operating Income: To track how effectively revenue growth is being converted into profit.

Intended Visualization Techniques:

  • Dual-Axis Comparison: A combination chart using bars for Physical Case Volume and a line for Net Sales to highlight the "Price/Mix" gap.
  • Trend Analysis: Line charts showing Gross Margin % over time to visualize the impact of raw material costs (aluminum, sugar, etc.) mentioned in the report.
